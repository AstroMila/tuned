The problem

You can't watch everything.

The best gigs, commissions, and roles for people who do scattered, project-based work aren't sitting on one platform — they're spread across festival call-outs, niche boards, cruise line postings, forwarded emails, and sites that don't even have an alert feature. Questions

Good to know.

Is this only for musicians and artists?

That's who it helps most — scattered, project-based work is exactly the mess Tuned is built to untangle. But it works for other kinds of work too. If you're not sure it fits yours, just ask.

Is this really AI doing the research?

Yes, openly. Every email says so. We think that's the right way to do this — the tedious searching is exactly the kind of work AI should be doing, and being upfront about it is how we take responsibility when it gets something wrong.

Which sites do you actually search?

The open web, public listings, and RSS-style feeds — plus anything you forward us, like a platform's own alert emails. We don't log into gated platforms or scrape accounts; several explicitly disallow that, and we're not going to do it quietly.

What if the first few emails aren't quite right?

That's expected — it's why the first month is guided. Tell us what missed the mark and we adjust it with you, not just once, but every month after that too.

Can I stop anytime?

Yes. Cancel whenever — no lock-in beyond whichever billing period you're in.
